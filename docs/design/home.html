Validate a JWT in C#, in a few readable lines.

JWT Validator hides the complexity of TokenValidationParameters, JwtSecurityTokenHandler and ClaimsPrincipal. Check the signature, issuer, audience and expiry, and get the claims back as a dictionary.

Why I built this

Over the years I have seen many developers trip up on the last step of an OAuth sign-in: validating the ID token. TokenValidationParameters, JwtSecurityTokenHandler and ClaimsPrincipal are powerful, but there is a lot to get right.

I built JWT Validator so that step takes a few readable lines, whether the token is signed with a secret or by an OpenID Connect provider.
